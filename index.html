Felix Reels

Felix Reels is a private tool that creates short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) for the owner's own YouTube channels "Creepy Fact" and "Жуткий факт" and uploads them after the owner approves each video.

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to channels owned by the operator of this app. It does not collect, store or share data of other users.
