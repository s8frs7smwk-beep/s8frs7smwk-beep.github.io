Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Felix Reels ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by Andrey Shubin for personal use.

What the app does

The app generates short videos and uploads them to YouTube channels and TikTok accounts owned by the app operator, using the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API. On YouTube the app requests permission to upload videos and read the channel name. On TikTok the app requests user.info.basic (to show the account name), video.upload (to send a video to the TikTok inbox as a draft) and video.publish (to publish a video the owner has approved).

Information we access

When the channel or account owner authorizes the app, Google or TikTok provides an OAuth token that allows uploading videos to that owner's channel or account. The app also stores the account's display name and TikTok open ID to show which account is connected. Tokens are stored privately on the app's server and are used only to upload videos the owner has approved.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of YouTube viewers or any other users. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers or account details.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

The app's use of TikTok APIs follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service. See also the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted.
