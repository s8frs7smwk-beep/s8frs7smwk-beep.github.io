Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Felix Reels ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by Andrey Shubin for personal use.

What the app does

The app generates short videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by the app operator, using the YouTube Data API. The app only requests the youtube.upload permission.

Information we access

When the channel owner authorizes the app, Google provides an OAuth token that allows uploading videos to that owner's channel. The token is stored privately on the app's server and is used only to upload videos the owner has approved.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of YouTube viewers or any other users. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers or account details.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted.
